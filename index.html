PERSONAL WORKSPACE · APP INFORMATION

Desk — Life OS

Desk is a personal workspace for tasks and calendar planning. This website provides information about its Google Calendar connection. The workspace itself runs locally on its owner's computer.

The Google connection currently requests read-only calendar access. Desk uses selected calendars to show busy time and help plan around existing appointments and scheduled task blocks. This connection does not edit or delete existing Google Calendar events.

Google sign-in lets the owner authorise access without giving Desk a Google password. Account access can be revoked in Google Account settings.

This public website contains no private workspace records, calendar events or sign-in credentials.
