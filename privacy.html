Privacy notice

Updated 18 October 2026 · Personal local calendar connection

Purpose and information used

Desk is a personal workspace operated by its owner. Its Google Calendar connection requests read-only access to calendars the signed-in account can access. Desk discovers the calendar list, then reads event timing and related scheduling metadata from calendars explicitly selected by the owner. The current reader does not request event titles, descriptions or attendees; it displays generic busy-time labels.

Storage and security

Google client details and connection tokens are stored encrypted in the local Desk application-data folder, outside the workspace and cloud-synced files. The encryption key is a separate file restricted to the current operating-system user; it is not a hardware-backed keystore. Desk does not collect or store Google passwords. Browser sign-in cookies are managed separately by the browser and Google.

Use and transmission

Calendar information supports calendar display, availability checks and planning. Desk exchanges sign-in tokens and calendar requests with Google. Busy-time information may be included in requests to the owner's configured AI provider when the owner uses assistant features. Calendar information is not published on this information website, sold, or used for advertising.

Control and retention

The owner chooses which calendars Desk uses and can forget a local connection in Desk. Forgetting removes its stored local connection, without deleting Google events or revoking Google's separate permission grant. The owner can revoke that grant through Google Account connections. Local connection details remain until forgotten or removed; Google may expire or revoke access. Other workspace records and saved assistant outputs are controlled separately by the owner.

This website and contact

This static information website has no app sign-in, calendar data, tracking scripts or advertising. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process ordinary web-request information under GitHub's privacy statement. For questions about the Google connection, use the developer contact address shown on Google's consent screen.
